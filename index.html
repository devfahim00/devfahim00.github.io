01 — About

Systems, not just screens.

I'm Fahim, a developer from Bangladesh. I design and ship whole ecosystems — the mobile app, the API behind it, the admin panel that runs it, and the bots and automation around it — instead of isolated features.

My work centers on streaming, proxy tooling, payment verification and Telegram-based products, with a focus on the Bangladeshi internet context: bKash payments, local content and tools that stay fast on real-world connections.
